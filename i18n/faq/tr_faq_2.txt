#q en: How do I use laminating and heat binding?
#a en
There are two kinds of laminators. Check which one your store has first.
⚫ Black PL-1506 (laminating only)
⚪ White SAFA-BM100 (laminating · binding in one)

👍 If the store has both, please use the PL-1506 first. It warms up and laminates faster.
📖 Follow the guide for your laminator in the top menu [사용방법] (How to Use) → [코팅] (Laminate).
💰 First check how many pouches there are, and pay first.

📚 How to use heat binding
Heat binding puts your prints in a cover and melts the glue inside the cover with heat to make them into a book. No hole punching or springs. Heat binding is done on the white combination machine (SAFA-BM100).
1. Choose a heat-binding cover that fits your number of sheets. You can put in only up to the number written on the cover.
2. Pay for the cover at the Toss kiosk. It's 1,500 won per cover.
3. Press the power button, then press the heat [온열] button once to warm it up. After about 3 minutes an alarm sounds and the light stops — warm-up is done.
4. Put the pages neatly so they touch the glue inside the cover, and push the cover, closed end first, deep into the long slot on top.
5. Press the timer and wait.
6. When it's done, take it out, cool it for 10+ minutes, then open it.
⚠️ Too few sheets (e.g. 5 or 10) can't be bound.
