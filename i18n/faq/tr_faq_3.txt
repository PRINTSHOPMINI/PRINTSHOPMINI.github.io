[[img:images/faq/pay-toss-s6-method-v2.png]]

#q en: Can I pay with Kakao Pay or Naver Pay? How?
#a en
✅ You can pay at the Toss kiosk. The card reader next to the printer machine accepts only credit/debit cards and Samsung Pay.
The way depends on the service you want to use.

🛍️ When paying for products (laminating pouches · heat-binding covers · L-folders · slide-binder files · document envelopes)
1. Press [결제하기] (Pay) at the Toss kiosk and choose the product.
2. For the payment method, press [QR 간편결제] (QR easy pay).
3. On your phone, open the payment QR code in the Kakao Pay (or Naver Pay) app.
4. Hold the QR code up to the kiosk screen (the camera at the top).
5. Wait until the payment is done.

🖨️ For printing · copying · scanning · fax
- Pay with [선불충전] (Prepaid top-up) at the Toss kiosk.
- Press [QR 간편결제] (QR easy pay) as the payment method to use Kakao Pay · Naver Pay.
- There are many steps, so be sure to read the guide with pictures. It's explained in detail in 'FAQ: I don't have a card. Can I pay with cash or bank transfer? (Toss kiosk prepaid top-up)'.
