#q en: How do I get a parking discount (parking registration)?
#a en
🅿️ Apply for a parking discount at '주차등록' (Parking registration).

🚫 Stores with no parking discount
- Gwonseon store (권선점)
- Dongbaek Station store (동백역점)
These two stores have parking lots, but they are paid.
PrintShop Mini does not give parking discounts there. 🙏

✅ For other stores, tap the picture below to apply.

📋 Each store has a different parking discount policy!
When you choose your store (branch) on the site, its parking discount policy appears.
Read the policy carefully before applying for the discount.

👇 The parking registration site opens.
[[btn:images/faq/btn-parking2.png|https://fax.printnuri.com/parking|Go to parking registration]]
💻 There is also a [주차할인] (Parking discount) icon on the store PC desktop. You can apply there too.

📝 Do this
1. In [지점명] (Store name), choose the store where you parked. Its parking discount info (time, conditions) appears. Please read it carefully.
2. In [카드 구분] (Card type), choose how you paid.
   - Samsung Pay · Apple Pay · kiosk payment (binding · laminating): enter the [승인번호(8자리)] (8-digit approval number).
   - Credit card (physical card): enter the [앞 4자리] (first 4 digits) and [뒤 4자리] (last 4 digits) of the card number.
3. Enter [차량번호(4자리)] (car number, 4 digits).
4. Press [검색] (Search). It may take a little while to find your car. Please wait until the screen changes.
5. Check that your car number, entry time and car photo are correct.
6. Press [주차할인적용] (Apply parking discount), and when "주차 할인권을 적용하시겠습니까?" (Apply the parking discount?) appears, press [주차할인적용] once more.
7. When "주차 할인권이 등록되었습니다" (Parking discount registered) appears, you're done.

🔎 Tap to see how to find the approval number.
[[go:faq.html#faq-6|How to find the Samsung Pay · Apple Pay approval number]]

⚠️ If you see these screens, do this
- "결제 정보를 찾을 수가 없습니다" (Payment info not found): check the store, card type and card number (approval number) again.
- "차량 정보를 찾을 수가 없습니다" (Car info not found): check the car number again.
- "결제하신 금액이 주차 할인을 받기 위한 최소 금액보다 적습니다" (Amount paid is less than the minimum): check the minimum payment in your store's parking discount info.
- "이미 주차 등록된 이력이 존재합니다" (Already registered): it's already registered with the same payment.
- "등록이 실패하였습니다" (Registration failed): tell us on TalkTalk as shown below.

⚠️ If registration doesn't work on the site, don't drive out — tell us on TalkTalk. (Phone also works weekdays 9 AM–9 PM)
✉️ What to send on TalkTalk
1. Store name
2. Contact number
3. Last 4 digits of the car number
4. The problem (parking discount site error)
⏳ Please wait in the store for a moment while a manager checks. The store isn't responsible for parking fees if you drive out first without contacting us.
💳 If you paid several times with the same card, you may be able to register them together. (Different cards can't be combined)

Hãy đọc kỹ chính sách rồi mới đăng ký giảm phí.

👇 Trang đăng ký gửi xe sẽ mở ra.
[[btn:images/faq/btn-parking2.png|https://fax.printnuri.com/parking|Đi đăng ký gửi xe]]

2. Ở [카드 구분] (Loại thẻ), chọn cách bạn đã thanh toán.

   - Thẻ tín dụng (thẻ vật lý): nhập [앞 4자리] (4 số đầu) và [뒤 4자리] (4 số cuối) của số thẻ.
3. Nhập [차량번호(4자리)] (biển số xe, 4 số).
4. Bấm [검색] (Tìm kiếm). Tìm xe có thể mất một chút thời gian. Hãy chờ đến khi màn hình thay đổi.
5. Kiểm tra biển số, giờ vào và ảnh xe có đúng không.
6. Bấm [주차할인적용] (Áp dụng giảm phí), khi hiện "주차 할인권을 적용하시겠습니까?" (Áp dụng phiếu giảm phí?) thì bấm [주차할인적용] thêm một lần nữa.

🔎 Muốn biết cách xem số phê duyệt, hãy bấm vào đây.

#q en: Refunds at a glance: what is and isn't refunded
#a en
📝 Please know this first
- For a refund you must file a [환불접수] (Refund request). Without filing, there is no refund.
- ⏰ You must file within 7 days of the problem. After 7 days, we can't process the refund.
- Filing doesn't mean an instant refund. A manager checks it, processes only eligible cases, and tells you the result separately at the contact you gave.
- Leave the problem print in the store when you file. If you don't leave it, there is no refund.
[[btn:images/faq/btn-refund2.png|https://fax.printnuri.com/refund|Go to refund request]]
💻 You can also file with the [환불신청] (Refund request) icon on the store PC desktop.

✅ Eligible for a refund (processed after a manager checks)
- Machine breakdown, paper jam, toner smudge
- Blank pages because the printer machine broke
- A long white line appears
- Colors are smudged
- A single color comes out like a gradient
- Shapes unrelated to the original are printed all over
- You paid but nothing printed after more than 10 minutes (don't pay again — contact us first)
- Pages that didn't print because it stopped with "no paper" (only the pages that didn't print)
- You chose the Color printer but it came out black and white (we partly cancel: the total minus the B&W price)
- The fax failed ([전송실패], Send failed) because the receiving number was wrong (we cancel the payment)

- It went wrong because you worked without reading the guide
- It went wrong because you misunderstood the guide

- You printed a USB file without saving it to the PC, and it came out blank
- You didn't save a compressed file to the PC, unzip it, and open the unzipped file by double-clicking before printing, so part or all came out blank
- You didn't save a file from email · cloud · web drive to the PC and open the saved file by double-clicking before printing, so it came out wrong
- The payment info screen showed B&W/color, two-sided/one-sided, paper size, pages and price before paying, but you paid without checking and then ask for a refund because it printed wrong

- Two-sided/one-sided
- Paper size

#q en: I paid and it was printing fine, then it suddenly said "no paper" and stopped printing.
#a en
😮 Was it printing fine and then suddenly stopped saying "no matching paper"?
This happens because the PDF file has a page with a different size.

🔎 Why does this happen?
- It can happen even if the sizes in the machine's list all look right (A4·B4·A3).
- Some page in the PDF may not be A4·B4·A3 size.
  e.g.) In a 10-page PDF, one of pages 2–10 has a different size
- When printing reaches that page, it says "no matching paper".
- It's hard to know in advance. You only find out when you actually print.

✅ How to prevent it (always do this before printing)
1. Open the PDF files one at a time.
2. In the print options, find 'PDF 페이지 크기에 따라 용지 소스 선택' (choose paper source by PDF page size).
3. Be sure to uncheck that box.
4. Send the print job again, starting from the part that didn't print.
5. Go to the printer machine and pay.
💡 When sending several files together, uncheck it in every PDF file.

💸 Already paid and some parts didn't print?
1. File a refund with the [환불신청] (Refund request) icon on the PC desktop.
2. For the payment amount, write what you actually paid.
3. For the refund amount, calculate and write the price of the parts that didn't print.
⏰ You must file within 7 days of the problem. If you didn't file, or filed after 7 days, there is no refund.
ℹ️ Filing doesn't mean an instant refund. A manager checks it, processes only eligible cases, and tells you the result at the contact you gave.

#q en: An Error window appears on the printer machine screen and it doesn't respond well.
#a en
This window appears if you hold the edge of the screen with your hand, or if something is placed on the screen.
[[img:images/faq/touch-error.png]]
⚠️ When this happens, the screen doesn't respond well.

Try this.
1. ✋ Take your hand off the edge of the screen.
2. 📦 Remove anything placed on the screen (phone · wallet · paper).
3. 👆 Tap buttons lightly with one finger.

❌ Don't hold or lean on the screen!
#q zh: 复合机屏幕上出现 Error 窗口，按不太动。
#a zh
用手抓着屏幕边框，或屏幕上放了东西，就会出现这个窗口。
[[img:images/faq/touch-error.png]]
⚠️ 这时屏幕不太好按。

请这样试试。
1. ✋ 把手从屏幕边框上拿开。
2. 📦 拿走放在屏幕上的东西（手机 · 钱包 · 纸）。
3. 👆 用一根手指轻轻按按钮。

❌ 不要抓着或靠在屏幕上！
#q vi: Màn hình máy in đa năng hiện cửa sổ Error và khó bấm.
#a vi
Cửa sổ này hiện ra khi bạn cầm vào viền màn hình, hoặc có đồ vật đặt trên màn hình.
[[img:images/faq/touch-error.png]]
⚠️ Lúc này màn hình khó bấm.

Hãy thử như sau.
1. ✋ Bỏ tay ra khỏi viền màn hình.
2. 📦 Lấy đi đồ vật đặt trên màn hình (điện thoại · ví · giấy).
3. 👆 Bấm nút nhẹ nhàng bằng một ngón tay.

❌ Đừng cầm hoặc tựa vào màn hình!
