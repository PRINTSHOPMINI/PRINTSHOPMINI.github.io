How to laminate COATING

Check how many pouches you need and pay first. Then turn on the power, wait for it to warm up, and feed in the pouch. Always turn the power off when you're done.

How to use the black laminator (PL-1506) PL-1506

This guide is for the Paper Friend PL-1506 laminator in [문서] (Document) mode. If your store has the black PL-1506, laminate this way first. (Stores with only the white SAFA-BM100: follow the SAFA-BM100 guide at the very bottom.)

  1. First check how many laminating pouches are on display.
  2. If there are enough, choose how many at the Toss kiosk and pay by card, then follow the steps below.
  3. If there are fewer pouches than you need, come back another time, or use only what's there.

  • 3
    READY light — the green light normally blinks, and also blinks while warming up. After about 60 seconds there is a "beep", and when it stops blinking and stays on, warm-up is done.

How to use the black PL-1506 laminator
1
Check the pouches · pay first!

First check how many laminating pouches are on display.
If there are enough, choose how many at the Toss kiosk and pay by card before using it.
If there are fewer pouches than you need, come back another time, or use only what's there.

⚠️ The order is check how many → pay → laminate. Always pay first
⚠️ Refunds for Toss kiosk payments are sent to a bank account. If you pay twice by your own mistake, we send back the amount minus the fee. Please don't pay twice.
2
Press the power button

Press the power button to turn it on. (power ON)

💡 Normal (default) state — the laminator is always plugged in, so the POWER red light keeps blinking and the READY green light also blinks. This is normal, not a breakdown.
3
Press the [문서] (Document) button

Press the [문서] (Document) button to set Document mode.
When the [문서] button's red light blinks and the READY green light also blinks, warm-up has started.
Don't press the [사진] (Photo) · [무열코팅] (Cold) buttons. Use Document mode only.

4
Wait for it to warm up

Warming up takes about 60 seconds.
After 60 seconds, you hear a "beep", and when the READY green light stops blinking and stays on, warm-up is done.
The red light by the [문서] button keeps blinking — that's normal. Don't put a pouch in while the READY green light is blinking.

5
Put in your print

Put your print inside the pouch.

6
Sealed end first, straight in

Feed the pouch in sealed end first, keeping it level.
The sealed end is the short (top) edge of the pouch. Not the long side.

7
Push until it's pulled in by itself

Push it in slowly and flat. The sensor at the slot detects the pouch, and the laminator pulls it in automatically.

8
Check it comes out

The pouch goes in the front slot and comes out the back. Check that it comes out the back.
⚠️ Never put anything in the back where pouches come out. Only use the front slot.

!Laminating safety — things not to do and things to do. Please follow them
🔥🚨 Fire risk — NEVER do this! 🚨🔥
🚫 Don't put in a print alone!Never put a print in the laminator without a pouch
🚫 Don't put in an empty pouch!Never put an empty pouch in the laminator without a print inside
⚠️ Doing either of these can start a fire inside the laminator. 🧯
Only put in a pouch with your print inside. There is a risk of fire and damage to the machine.
Do this
  • Feed the pouch sealed end first. (Must)
  • Don't leave it resting in the slot. Carefully, without wrinkling it, push it in slowly until it's pulled in automatically.
  • For several sheets, wait until the first pouch comes out before putting in the next one.
    ⚠️ Pouches go in the front slot and come out the back. Don't put anything in the back where they come out.

    Put ① the first pouch in the front slot.

    Wait ② until it comes all the way out the back.

    ③ Check that it's all the way out, take it out, then put the next pouch in the front slot.

  • When you're done, press the power button to turn it off.
Don't do this
  • Don't put in a print cut smaller than the pouch. If there is a lot of empty space in the pouch (A4 · A3), those parts stick together with no paper inside and come out crumpled and wrinkled.
    Especially if the print doesn't reach the sealed end, the corner of the sealed end can crumple and get stuck inside the laminator, or come out crumpled.
    👉 Use a print that fits the pouch size and reaches the sealed end.
  • Prints you brought from outside can't be used. (Only paper from our store)
  • Laminating pouches you brought can't be used. (Only pouches from our store)
  • Don't press any buttons while the laminator is running. (Pressing the wrong one causes problems)
※ These warnings apply to all laminators, both the white SAFA-BM100 and the black PL-1506.

How to use the white SAFA-BM100 laminator
🔀 This machine (SAFA-BM100) is a laminating · binding machine in one
🚫 The long slot on top is only for binding covers. Never put a laminating pouch in the top slot!
Laminating pouches go only in the lower (front) slot. A pouch in the top slot can get stuck and break the machine.
🧾 Before you start laminating! Same for white SAFA-BM100 · black PL-1506

  2. If there are enough, choose how many at the Toss kiosk and pay by card, then follow the steps below.

1
Check the pouches · pay first!

First check how many laminating pouches are on display.
If there are enough, choose how many at the Toss kiosk next to the machine and pay by card before using it.
If there are fewer pouches than you need, come back another time, or use only what's there.

⚠️ The order is check how many → pay → laminate. Always pay first
⚠️ Refunds for Toss kiosk payments are sent to a bank account. If you pay twice by your own mistake, we send back the amount minus the fee. Please don't pay twice.
2
Power on · warm up

Press the power button, then press the laminating button twice.
The light blinks, and after 3–5 minutes it beeps and the light stays on. Warm-up is done.

⚠️ If you pressed the laminating button 3 or more times, press the power button to turn it off, then start again: press power, then the laminating button twice.
3
Put your print in the pouch

Put your print neatly inside the laminating pouch.

4
Feed it into the laminator

Feed the pouch into the slot sealed end first.
Keep it level and straight, without wrinkling.

5
Watch it come out

Watch that the pouch comes out the back smoothly without getting caught.

6
Power OFF

When you're done, always turn the power off.

If you don't turn it off, it causes problems for the next customer.
