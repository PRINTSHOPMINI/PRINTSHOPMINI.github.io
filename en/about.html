OUR STORY

The PrintShop Mini
story

It began in 2021 in the new city of Gwanggyo. With the Gyeonggi Provincial Office and public offices moving in, and Gwanggyo Jungang Station opening on the Shinbundang Line, young people were moving in fast and Gwanggyo was growing into a lively new city. With nearby universities and large apartment complexes drawing people in their 20s to 40s, PrintShop Mini was born from the belief that this city needed a print service people could use conveniently at any time.

At first it was only A4 black-and-white printing, but by listening to customers we grew to 6 services: copy, scan, fax, laminate and bind.

Today we run 16 stores in 4 areas (수원·화성·용인·평택), used by thousands of customers every month.
