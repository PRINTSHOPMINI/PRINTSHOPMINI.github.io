SERVICE 01

Print

A self-printing service: send your file from the PC, then find it at the printer machine with a 4-digit PIN and print. B&W, color and certificate printing are all available, and you can pay by card (IC, Samsung Pay).

B&W A470 won/side
Color A4250 won/side
B&W A3140 won/side
Color A3500 won/side
See how to print →
📋
SERVICE 02

Copy

A self-copying service: put your original in the printer machine and a paper copy comes out. (Unlike scanning, which makes a file, copying gives you paper.) You choose settings yourself, like B&W/color, A4/A3, one-sided/two-sided, enlarge/reduce, and pay by card.

B&W A470 won/side
Color A4250 won/side
B&W A3140 won/side
Color A3500 won/side
See how to copy →
📄
SERVICE 03

Scan

A self-scanning service that turns your original into a file on the printer machine. You can save the scanned file to a USB or send it by email from a store PC.

Scan A4100 won/side
Scan A3100 won/side
See how to scan →

Fax

A self-fax service that scans your original and sends it to the other person's fax number. You can check the result with a QR code.
