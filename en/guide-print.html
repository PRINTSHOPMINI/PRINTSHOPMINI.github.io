How to print PRINT

Open your file on the store PC and print it on paper. First open your file on the PC and send the print job. Then go to the printer machine, find your document with your PIN, and pay. Then it prints.
⚠️ After you finish on the PC, go to the printer machine. Find your document with the PIN you typed on the PC, and pay. Only then does it print. Nothing comes out until you pay at the printer machine's card reader.
※ Even if you already paid a fee on the PC (e.g. for issuing a document), you must pay again for printing at the printer machine's card reader.

▶Watch the printing video first No sound needed
🌐 You can change the printer machine screen to English
  1. On the printer machine's first screen, tap [한국어] at the top right.
  2. Choose English from the list. The screen changes to English.
  3. When you're done, please switch back to [한국어]. Sometimes it doesn't switch back by itself.
🔢 Printing order · same for all file types Always in this order!
  1. 1
    🖨️ Choose the printer first.
    Pick the [흑백출력] (B&W) or [컬러출력] (Color) printer before anything else.
  2. 2
    ⚙️ Set the print options.
    Paper size · one-sided/two-sided · copies · pages, etc.
  3. 3
    👀 Check the preview.
    Make sure nothing is missing, and that it's in color if you chose color.
  4. 4
    ✅ Finally, press the [인쇄] (Print) button.
    Depending on the program, it's the [인쇄] button, a printer icon, or the [확인] (OK) button.
🚫 Don't change the printer after setting the options!

If you change the printer after setting the options, you must set all the options again. Changing the printer changes all the options.

❌ This is the most risky case
You set the options without choosing a printer → later you change from B&W to Color → the options changed but you don't check → the options are applied strangely and the print comes out wrong.

✅ Always: choose printer → set options → preview → print button. If you changed the printer, check the options again from the start.

Printing tips

Things people often miss when printing.

🎨 Check color in the preview!

If you chose the Color printer [컬러출력], make sure the preview is also in color. If there are many pages, check every page.

🖍️ Colors may look different

Printed colors can be different from the PC screen. Even in the same file, colors can differ from page to page.

🧾 Check the payment info screen before paying!

Before you pay, the printer machine shows the [Billing Information] screen. If it is different from what you expect, do NOT pay. Check the print options on the PC again.

Next · Set print options
  1. 1
    Choose printer: In the printer list, click the printer you want ([흑백출력] (B&W) or [컬러출력] (Color)).
  2. 2
    Settings button: Press [설정] (Settings) and the [프린터 속성] (Printer Properties) window opens. It has a [기본] (Basic) tab and a [용지] (Paper) tab. Check 2-1 and 2-2 below in order.
    2-1 [기본] (Basic) tab — orientation · one-sided/two-sided
    • A
      Orientation: Choose [세로] (Portrait) or [가로] (Landscape).
    • B
      Two-sided: For one side only choose [사용안함] (Off). For two sides choose [긴면 묶음] (long edge) or [짧은면 묶음] (short edge).
    2-2 [용지] (Paper) tab — paper size
    • A
      Original size [원본 문서 크기]: Choose the paper size you want (A4 · B4 · A3).
    • B
      Output size [출력 문서 크기]: Choose the same size as A here too. Don't leave it as [원본 문서 크기와 동일] (same as original).
    📌 e.g.) To print on A4, choose A4 in both A and B.
    ⚠️ For some files, if you don't set [출력 문서 크기] (Output size), the printer machine shows the Hangul file's size, such as Letter or Custom.
  3. 3
    Set the number of copies.
  4. 4
    Choose the pages to print. Choose [전체] (All), or to print only some pages choose [일부분] (Some) and type the page numbers.
  5. 5
    Pages per side: For one page per side choose [기본 인쇄] (Normal). For several pages on one side choose [모아 찍기] (N-up) and type the number.
  6. 6
    When all settings are done, press the [인쇄] (Print) button.
    ※ This is a shared PC, so print settings change every time. Check and set all the options you want before printing.
💡 The fonts are different from the PC where the file was made, so fonts, line spacing or tables can shift, or symbols (□) not in the original can appear. In Hangul, use [다른 이름으로 저장] (Save As) → PDF and bring that, and it prints just like the original.
That's all for Hangul print options! Press [인쇄] (Print) and the PIN box appears.Continue from Step 2 (type a 4-digit PIN) of ‘How to print’ above ⬆

How to print a TOEIC certificate TOEIC · TOEIC Speaking

For TOEIC · TOEIC Speaking (YBM) certificates, first print on the PC with [증명서전용PIN9999] (certificate printer). If you choose another printer, the PC freezes!

⚠️ Just issuing (paying) on the website does not print it Please read this

A certificate comes out only after you ① send it to print on the PC with [증명서전용PIN9999], then ② find it at the printer machine with number 9999 and pay.
If you don't send it on the PC and only type 9999 at the printer machine, nothing comes out.

1
Open the certificate on the website

When you get your score report on the TOEIC website, the certificate opens in a viewer window.

Picture guides for university transcripts, graduation certificates and Certpia are coming soon. For those certificates too, choose the [증명서전용PIN9999] printer.
2
Press [성적표 출력] (Print score report)

Press the yellow [성적표 출력] (Print score report) at the top right of the viewer.
[테스트 출력] (Test print) is only for testing. Don't press it.

3
Printer: only [증명서전용PIN9999]!

In the printer window, choose [증명서전용PIN9999] and press [인쇄] (Print).

⚠️ If you choose [흑백출력] (B&W) · [컬러출력] (Color), the PC freezes! Then it's hard to get back the certificate fee. Only choose [증명서전용PIN9999].
4
Done when it says printing is finished!

[증명서전용PIN9999] doesn't show a PIN box. 9999 is entered automatically, so this is normal.
When it says printing is complete, you're done on the PC.

5
Find it at the printer machine with number 9999

At the printer machine, press [Print] → [PC] and type 9999.
When the list appears, choose only your certificate, press [Start], then pay.

⚠️ Other customers may have used 9999 too, so several documents may be in the list. Check the time and name and pay only for yours. Don't press [Print All]. Everything in the list would be paid for.
Not sure what to do at the printer machine?See ‘Printing at the printer machine’ below ⬇

Printing at the printer machine Same for Edge · Chrome · ALSee

Do these steps after you type the PIN on the PC and send the print job. It's the same for Edge, Chrome and ALSee.

M1
On the printer machine: Print → PC → type the number

Go to the printer machine and press [Print] → [PC].
In the PIN box, type the 4-digit number you used on the PC and touch [OK].

M2
Check your document list

When the list appears, carefully check what it shows: document name, time, B&W/color, two-sided/one-sided, pages, paper size, etc.
Make sure it is your document and the print options are what you wanted.

M3
Touch the list and [Start]

Touch your document in the list and it turns blue. Then touch [Start].
If there are several, touch only the ones to print so they turn blue, then press [Start]. Only the ones you chose are paid for and printed together.
Press [Print All] at the top right only when everything in the list is yours. The whole list is paid for and printed at once.

M4
Check the price [Billing Information]

Finally, the [Billing Information] screen appears. Check everything carefully, and if the price is right too, touch [OK].

⚠️ If the pages or price on the payment info screen are different from what you expect, do NOT pay.
🧾 You must check the [Billing Information] screen!Check everything on the payment info screen. If you pay without checking and something is wrong, there is no refund or compensation.
Press [OK] to pay only after checking everything and it is all correct.
M5
Card payment

When the card reader speaks, insert your IC card (credit/debit), or hold Samsung Pay to it.

💳 Insert your credit/debit card into the card reader slot, chip side in.
